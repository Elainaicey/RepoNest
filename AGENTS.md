# RepoNest project guidance

## Visual language and color

- Treat previously mentioned colors such as white, cherry-blossom pink, violet, sky blue, and khaki as examples of the desired fresh and modern feeling, not as a fixed or exhaustive palette.
- Use the full Radix Themes color system as the primary reference when choosing colors: <https://www.radix-ui.com/themes/docs/theme/color>.
- Select hues by product context and semantic role. Any suitable Radix family may be used, including warm, cool, earthy, vivid, or tinted-neutral families, provided the result remains cohesive and refined.
- Prefer a deliberate scale-based system over isolated color values: subtle steps for page and component backgrounds, middle steps for interactive surfaces and borders, solid steps for primary actions and indicators, and the darkest readable steps for text.
- Pair accents with an appropriate tinted neutral such as gray, mauve, slate, sage, olive, or sand instead of defaulting to pure gray.
- Preserve accessible contrast, visible focus states, clear status semantics, and equivalent visual hierarchy in both light and dark themes. Alpha colors may be used for natural overlays and translucent panels.
- Avoid turning the interface into an indiscriminate rainbow. Color variety should strengthen hierarchy, meaning, rhythm, and delight while keeping RepoNest calm, fresh, and premium.
- These rules are design guidance, not a requirement to add Radix Themes as a runtime dependency. Reuse its principles and token anatomy within the project's existing styling approach unless adopting the library is separately justified.
